The ideaEvery value in sections by topic, with the base and this build side by side. Tap any value to see what changed it (Rush hit 1 is open here): the base hit, then each capsule, skill or Sparking effect. The sheet is the quick view, and this is the deep dive.
This roundCharge levels sit under their attack (Charge Lv.1 to 3, as many as the attack has). Sparking-only moves and traits (Hyper Smash, Burst Rush, Violent Rush, extra follow-ups) have their own Sparking! Mode section. New from the game files (marked NEW): charged, jump and dash ki blasts; smash and Heavy Finish charge levels; a Mobility section with movement ki costs; the Moveset (Rush Chains and their follow-ups, in the game's own names); and Traits. A long section folds to its main rows with "Show all". Another character's traits read like "Hyper armor", "No Violent Rush", "Laser ki blasts" or "Can't fly". Breaking armor (new) shows which attacks break each kind of armor, normally and in Sparking!. Rush Chains (the game's name; it was Moveset) now sits after Melee in a rose of its own: each chain type with the Rush Chains that lead to it, its charge levels, then each follow-up on its own row with its total and hits (follow-ups are separate routes off the chain, as are Grand Slash's). The Command list (new) gives every skill, blast and ultimate a card of its own, in rows of Skills, Blasts and Ultimates (up to three a row, sharing the width): the main type by the damage (a skill's duration), the base and the boosted damage (one extra bar spent while firing) under it, the cost in the header, category, type and traits, then everything else the data has (ki to start, impact power, lunge speed, time limit, clashes, lock-on, guard, duration, activation, and a skill's effects). Melee no longer repeats the Heavy Finish (it's under Rush Chains) or Perfect Smash (always the Lv.3 smash). Combat limits gives the follow-up limit and each pursuit: Videl and Mr. Satan read Vanishing Attack No, Lightning Attack No, Dragon Homing Yes (Videl's has its own limit, 2).

The ideaPicking a fighter and building capsules, the two things the sheet opens. Character picker (from the nameplate's "Change"): the whole roster as faces like the game's select screen, in the game's order with DP on each, filtered by search, team (this season's lists), class and DP. Each face sits on its class's colour; Vegeta's own versions of a class (light blue), the android sub-types, Mr. Satan's and Yajirobe's, and the classes whose colour would be too close to another's (Villain, Secret, Legendary Super Saiyan) add a second colour as a faint tint on the name strip. The class filter chips carry the same colours, so they double as the legend. Hovering or tapping a face previews it on the right (art, class, DP, headline stats with their roster bars, its forms) before you pick, so you can browse without losing your sheet. From Compare or Vs opponent the same picker fills one side, and its button says which ("Pick for the right side"). Capsule picker (from the hexes): your build on the left (seven slots, the budget, the attack-boost cap, rule warnings, and what equipped capsules do that the stats can't show), every allowed capsule on the right by category, each with what it changes on this character's build, e.g. Rush 5-hit 2,523 → 2,648.
NotesFaces are the game's own icons; the preview art is the card art the sheet uses. The budget bar shows equipped capsules solid and the hovered one dashed (Rush Attack Boost 2: 2 → 5 of 20), and the same for the attack cap (Rush, Smash and Blast Attack Boosts, 6 points in Standard). A capsule that would go over the budget or the cap shows red and can't be placed; banned capsules stay hidden, with "Show banned" to list them. Effects that only work in Sparking! are tagged In Sparking!; effects the calculator can't show (charge times, combo scaling) are tagged Not in the stats. Numbers are real, from the engine (Goku (Z - Early) with Rush Attack Boost 1 and Blast Burst).
