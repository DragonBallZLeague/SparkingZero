Concept B: the in-game character sheet

The four views of concept B as quick static mockups, after your third round of changes. The numbers are real, from the published data and the engine. Only the switcher below works. On the real pages every dotted number opens its breakdown (Full stats shows one open).

1. Sheet 2. Full stats 3. Compare 4. Vs opponent
This roundA thin strip under a stat shows how it sits against the league: the tick is the league average, the dot is this character, green when better and red when worse. Health, starting ki and auto-recharge go without one, since everyone knows their averages. The Ki charge bar works the same way, slowest to fastest. Ki regen shows only the bars ki refills on its own. Wording uses "per" (0.7s per bar), "Ki blasts" is "Ki blast limit", and the ultimate is ULT.
Ultimate BurstWith Ultimate Burst equipped, the ultimate's cost gains a red burnout bar:Goku's build here has Blast Burst instead, which adds the outlined fourth ki bar to each blast.
