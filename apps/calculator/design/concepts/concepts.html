Calculator concepts

Three directions for the character view, before any detailed demo. Every one leads with the selected character. The numbers are real (Goku (Z - Early) with Rush Attack Boost 1 and Blast Burst; Vegeta (Z - Scouter); league medians over 241 characters), but these are static pictures: only the switcher below works. Dotted numbers open their breakdown in the real build; A shows one open.

A · Today's look, re-laid out B · In-game character sheet C · Versus screen
The ideaKeep the calculator's look (banded sections, monospace numbers, portrait with the class gradient, Base / Mod) but put the character first: a hero with the quick view in it, then stats and moves side by side, capsules on the right. The character list folds into a rail of faces.
FixesThe squeezed Skills column at 1280px, three panels competing for width, and the phone opening on the list. Tapping any number shows its parts.
Trade-offThe least change, so it is familiar, and the cheapest to build. It still reads as a dense data panel rather than a character showcase.

The ideaA visual overhaul in Sparking Zero's own menu style: angled plates, a large cut-out portrait, and stats drawn as the game draws them: HP in 10,000-HP bars, ki in bars, the skill gauge in points, moves as a command list with ki pips. The capsule loadout sits under the portrait like the game's equip screen.
FixesIt reads as a character, not a spreadsheet. Bars make the scale obvious (4 HP bars vs 5, 2 of 5 ki at the start), and the loadout is in view while you read the stats it changes.
Trade-offThe biggest build, and more vertical space per number. The full stat list moves behind a "Full stats" tab. It looks like the game, so it must stay readable rather than flashy.

The ideaA versus screen, so every number has something to be read against. The character is on the left. The right side is the league median by default, or a second character (Compare), or an opponent (Vs opponent: damage after their defenses). Bars grow out from the middle, and the larger side is the winner.
Fixes"Is 2,460 rush good?" is answered on the page (the league median is 2,706). Compare and Opponent stop being separate layouts: they only change what sits on the right.
Trade-offThe best fit for the "who is better, who to pick up" questions. Single-character reading is less direct, because every row is a comparison. Moves and capsules go in a side panel or a tab.
