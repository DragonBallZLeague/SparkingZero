Alternate blasts and ultimates in Versus

Ways to pick which version of a slot each fighter compares, so one fighter's alternate (a costume's move) can be set against the other's main move. Android 18's B2 has an alternate (Chain Destructo Disc), and so does Goku's ultimate (Legendary Strike). Every mock is clickable. Each is shown at desktop width and phone width.

0. Today for reference

An "Alternate ⇄" button under the move's name, which reads "Main ⇄" once switched.

The league didn't like it: the button looks like a separate control and makes the row taller.

A. Arrows on the name cycle in place

A move that has another version gets small ‹ › arrows around its name, with "1 of 2" beside it. A click flips to the next version, like flipping through forms in the game's character select.

Good: it stays on one line, and the arrows say "there's more than one" without a word. Less good: you don't see what the other version is until you flip to it.

B. Version marks under the name like page dots

Thin slanted marks under the name, one per version; the alternate's mark is orange when it's chosen. Click a mark to pick that version.

Good: the quietest option, and the count shows at a glance. Less good: the marks are small to click, and new users may not guess what they do without a tooltip.

C. A menu on the name pick from a list

The name becomes a drop-down (with ▾) only when the slot has another version. The menu lists every version with its damage, so you see both before picking.

Good: the clearest about what the options are, and it handles any number of versions. Less good: one more click than A or B, and a menu over the rows.

D. One switch per fighter Main / Costume above the rows

A Main / Costume switch for each fighter at the top of Blasts, shown only for a fighter who has a costume move. It swaps every alternate that fighter has at once (in practice nearly always one).

Good: the rows stay clean, and the switch reads like picking a costume in the game. Less good: it can't mix a costume's B2 with the main ultimate, which only matters for the few fighters with two costume moves.

E. Both versions listed tap one to compare it

The row lists every version with its damage; the one being compared is bright and boxed, the other faded. A click on the faded one makes it the compared one.

Good: nothing hidden: every number is on the page. Less good: the rows with alternates get taller, and two numbers per side are more to read.
